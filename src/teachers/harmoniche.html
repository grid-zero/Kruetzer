Biography

About Harmoniche

Harmoniche Deng is a first-year medical student studying at the Australian National University (ANU). Hailing from Perth, Western Australia, he began his piano studies under Luciano Trebse, developing a foundation in classical performance, completing Grade 8 Piano through the Australian Music Examinations Board (AMEB).

Teaches
Piano

Alongside his medical studies, Harmoniche has remained actively engaged in music through frequent background and community performances, including charity events and public functions, while continuing to cultivate a deeper artistic affinity for classical piano. In 2026, he made his classical performance debut in the Friends of the School of Music Classical Ensemble Competition at the ANU School of Music, marking an important milestone in his artistic development as a performer.

Balancing the demands of medicine with music has shaped Harmoniche's perspective on performance and expression. He views music not only as an artistic discipline, but also as a deeply human and restorative form of communication that parallels the healing vocation of medicine. Through performance, he seeks to explore the emotional and reflective dimensions shared between music, wellbeing, and human connection.
