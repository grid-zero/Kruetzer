Biography

About Phoebe

Phoebe is a pianist and collaborative musician currently undertaking a double degree in Law and Music at the Australian National University. Originally from Sydney, she began her musical training at a young age and has since developed a versatile musical foundation across piano, harpsichord, cello, violin, clarinet and classical voice.

Training

A musical foundation

Phoebe's musical training began under Dorine Tolley, where she developed her foundational studies in both piano and harpsichord. She went on to study piano with Natalia Ricci through the Sydney Conservatorium of Music's Rising Stars program, being among the youngest students accepted, before furthering her studies with Alexey Koltakov, Natalia Raspopova, Roderick MacFarlane, Ernest So and Tony Lee. She has received multiple awards in state and national competitions. In 2022, she completed Licentiate diplomas in Piano Performance with Trinity College London and the AMEB (Australian Music Examinations Board), and received substantial scholarship offers from Berklee College of Music (USA) and the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland.

Today

Performing & composing

Currently, she is a 2026 Canberra Symphony Orchestra Kingsland Fellow for piano.

In addition to her performance training, Phoebe is majoring in composition and film scoring at the Australian National University, which deepens her engagement with arranging and sound design- themes that lies in the heart of her teaching pedagogy

Philosophy

Teaching approach

Phoebe believes strong foundations set students free. Lessons balance careful technical work with plenty of real music, so students build both the skills and the love of playing that keep them going for life.

She listens closely, explains clearly and adapts to every learner — whether that's a young beginner who needs games and stickers, or an adult returning to the piano after decades away.
