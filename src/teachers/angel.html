Flute, Piano & Voice

Angel Zheng

Biography

About Angel

Angel Zheng is a New Zealand-born Chinese Wesley Scholar, a pianist, flautist and chorister currently undertaking a Bachelor of Political Science and Music, majoring in Piano Performance at the Australian National University. She is a music teacher, and holds an ATCL diploma in Flute.

Teaches
Flute, piano, voice

Experience

Performer & collaborator

She has extensive experience as a performer and collaborator across solo, chamber, orchestral, and choral contexts, working as pianist, flautist, singer, and student conductor. She has also trained extensively with the Aotearoa New Zealand Academy Choir, engaging in high-level national and international programmes, workshops, and performances. Alongside this, she has held multiple leadership roles, including sectional leadership, ensemble coordination, and conducting, where she developed strong communication, rehearsal direction, and organisational skills, as well as the ability to support and unify performers of varying experience levels.

Recognition

Achievements

Her major achievements include a Gold Diploma Level 5 at the World Choir Games with the Aotearoa New Zealand Academy Choir (2024), First Place in the BACH International Music Competition in the Chamber Music category (2024), BOYA International Piano Festival National Finals Silver Award (2018), the Friends of the ANU School of Music Ensemble Prizes (People’s Choice 2025; First Prize 2026), and grants from the Friends of the School of Music supporting her musical studies. She is also a recipient of the Llewellyn Choir Awards Programme as a Soprano.

Philosophy

Let the music win

Angel is particularly passionate about collaborative music-making and ensemble performance, guided by the philosophy to “let the music win.” She values musical sensitivity, adaptability, and the shared process of creating music within a group, and is committed to continued artistic growth through diverse performance experiences and collaboration. Her musical training has also strengthened her interpretative awareness, technical control, and sensitivity to balance and texture in ensemble settings.
