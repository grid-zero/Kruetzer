Biography

About Tyler

Tyler is a Canberra based pianist who is currently taking a double degree at the ANU studying a bachelor of Economics and Music, majoring in piano performance. Staying in Canberra his whole life, he has experienced a multitude of teachers whilst studying piano but has also picked up guitar in his early teens. Aside from performance, he also shares a passion for composition and has finished multiple arrangements for both piano and orchestral works.

Training

Shaped by many teachers

Finishing the AMEB certificate of performance in 2020, Tyler has been teaching since then both publicly and privately and has had extensive experience teaching all ages. He was also awarded the ACT BSSS ‘Excellence in Music’ award in 2022 upon graduating college.

Originally starting out with the late Jenni Lavett and later studying under Summer Liu for the AMEB grades, Tyler is now currently with Ella Luhtasaari who has given him extensive help on piano performance, theory and also teaching pedagogy. Throughout his higher studies, he has also worked with renowned pianists such as Scott Davie and Edward Neeman. Tyler has picked up varying teaching pedagogies from the many tutors he has been under and holds a great passion in demonstrating these methods to ensure the best learning experience.
