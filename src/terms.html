Conditions of tuition

We are committed to providing high-quality, one-on-one music education. Our approach is professional, structured and tailored to every individual student.

  • Lesson duration: Length and pricing are determined by student level — typically 30 minutes for beginners, 45 minutes for Grades 5–8, and 60 minutes or more for diploma students.
  • Practice: Regular practice is essential for progress. Limited progress due to lack of practice does not constitute grounds for a refund.
  • Materials: Students are responsible for purchasing their own books and sheet music. Your teacher will advise on what's needed.

Cancellations & make-up lessons

  • Notice: A minimum of 24 hours' notice is required to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows are charged in full with no make-up lesson.
  • Make-up lessons: One (1) make-up lesson is allowed per term. Evidence of misadventure or 24 hours' notice is required.
  • Teacher absence: If your teacher must cancel, a make-up lesson or full credit will always be offered.

Payment information

Lessons must be paid in advance in packages of 1, 5 or 10 lessons, or per term. Payments are accepted via bank transfer, PayID or cash. No refunds are provided if a student discontinues mid-package.

Terms & holidays

Lessons follow the ACT public school term calendar. No lessons are held on public holidays; these weeks are not charged. Holiday lessons may be arranged by request for exam or performance preparation.

Safety & wellbeing

  • Illness: Students who are unwell should not attend in person. An online lesson may be offered instead.
  • Working with Vulnerable People: All teachers hold a valid Working With Vulnerable People registration in the ACT.
  • Supervision: Parents are responsible for children before and after their lesson time.

Privacy & media

Your personal information is used only to manage lessons and communicate with you, and is never shared with third parties. Photos or recordings of students will only be shared publicly with written consent from the student or their parent or guardian.
