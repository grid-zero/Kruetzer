Biography

About Phoebe

Phoebe began piano at the age of five and has never stopped exploring what the instrument can say.

After completing Licentiate diplomas in Piano Performance with both Trinity College London and the AMEB, she went on to perform as a soloist and collaborative pianist in recitals, competitions and chamber concerts across Australia.

Teaching soon became her greatest passion. In 2014 she founded Kreutzer to offer the kind of tuition she believes every student deserves: rigorous, encouraging and deeply personal. Phoebe is committed to high-quality, one-on-one music education, specialising in students from beginner through to AMEB diploma level.

Philosophy

Teaching approach

Phoebe believes strong foundations set students free. Lessons balance careful technical work with plenty of real music, so students build both the skills and the love of playing that keep them going for life.

She listens closely, explains clearly and adapts to every learner — whether that's a young beginner who needs games and stickers, or an adult returning to the piano after decades away.

  • AMEB & Trinity exam preparation, all grades
  • Diploma coaching (AMusA, LMusA, ATCL, LTCL)
  • Theory & aural training
  • Competition and eisteddfod preparation
