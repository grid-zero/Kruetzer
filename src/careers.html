Our people

Who we're looking for

We look for teachers who combine strong musicianship with genuine warmth. You'll hold a tertiary music qualification or performance diploma, have experience teaching students of different ages, and believe that every learner can flourish with the right guidance.

Above all, you'll be someone students look forward to seeing each week.

  • Bachelor of Music or equivalent performance diploma
  • At least two years of private teaching experience
  • Current ACT Working with Vulnerable People registration (or willingness to obtain)
  • Familiarity with AMEB and/or Trinity syllabuses
  • Clear communication with students and parents
  • A love of sharing music
