Getting started

How do I book my free trial lesson?

Head to our contact page and fill out the short enquiry form. We'll get back to you within 24 hours to find a time that suits you at our Canberra studio.

What instruments do you teach?

We currently offer private lessons in piano, violin, cello, flute and voice, as well as music theory and musicianship. See our lessons page for details on each.

What age can my child start?

Most children are ready for piano or violin from around five years old. Flute is usually best from about eight, and voice from around ten. Adults are always welcome — it's never too late to start.

Do I need my own instrument?

For your trial lesson we can usually provide an instrument in the studio. For regular lessons you'll need one at home to practise on — we're happy to advise on buying or renting good-quality instruments.

Where are lessons held?

Our dedicated studio is in Canberra. Once your lesson is confirmed we'll send you the full address and parking details. Online lessons are also available.

Lessons & learning

Do students have to sit exams?

Not at all. Many students enjoy the structure of AMEB or Trinity exams, but they're entirely optional. We'll set goals that motivate you, whether that's a certificate, a performance or a favourite piece.

How much should I practise?

Little and often is best. Young beginners might practise 10–15 minutes a day; advanced students closer to an hour. Your teacher will give you a clear, realistic practice plan each week.

Can parents sit in on lessons?

Yes — especially for young beginners, a parent in the room helps practice at home. There's also a comfortable waiting area if you'd prefer to step out.

Are there performance opportunities?

Absolutely. We hold mid-year and end-of-year recitals, plus studio masterclasses and exam rehearsal days throughout the year.

Fees & policies

How much do lessons cost?

A 30-minute lesson is $60 on its own, or as little as $50 when booked as a 10-lesson pack. See the full fee schedule for longer lessons and advanced rates.

What if I need to cancel a lesson?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule. One make-up lesson is available per term. Full details are in our terms and conditions.

How do I pay?

Lessons are paid in advance by bank transfer, PayID or cash. We'll send an invoice at the start of each term or package.
