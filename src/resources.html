Practice guide

Practise smarter

Five habits our teachers recommend for every student, at every level.

Little and often

Fifteen focused minutes every day beats two hours once a week. Consistency builds muscle memory.

Slow is fast

Practise tricky passages slowly and accurately. Speed comes naturally once the notes are secure.

Start in the middle

Don't always begin at bar one. Isolate the hardest section first, while you're fresh.

Listen back

Record yourself on a phone. You'll hear things you never notice while playing.

End with joy

Finish every session with a piece you love. It's why we play in the first place.

Exams explained

Understanding AMEB grades

The Australian Music Examinations Board offers a clear pathway from first steps to professional-level diplomas. Exams are optional, but many students enjoy the goals and recognition they bring.

Your teacher will let you know when you're ready to enter — typically around one grade per year.

Prelim – 2
Foundations. Reading, basic technique and short, characterful pieces.
Grades 3 – 5
Developing. Broader repertoire, scales across the instrument, growing musicality.
Grades 6 – 8
Advanced. Substantial works and a theory requirement for certificates.
Diplomas
AMusA, LMusA, FMusA. Recital-length programmes at a professional standard.
