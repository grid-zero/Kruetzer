Our story

About Kreutzer

A Canberra studio where serious teaching and the simple joy of making music sit side by side.

Beginnings

Founded in Canberra

Kreutzer was founded in 2025 by Phoebe Mu, a pianist and collaborative musician studying Law and Music at the Australian National University.

Today Kreutzer teaches students across two Canberra studios, and we're building a community of musicians who learn from and alongside each other. In time, we hope to bring everyone together for a recital.

We named the studio after Rodolphe Kreutzer, the French violinist and teacher whose études are still studied by students everywhere — and to whom Beethoven dedicated his famous "Kreutzer" Sonata. His legacy reminds us that great teaching echoes for generations.

Philosophy

How we teach

Four ideas shape every lesson at Kreutzer, whatever the instrument or level.

Foundations first

Healthy technique, good posture and confident reading — so progress never stalls later on.

Musicianship, not just notes

Theory, aural skills and history woven naturally into lessons, so students understand what they play.

Goals that motivate

Exams, eisteddfods or simply a favourite piece — we set goals that matter to each student.

Joy in the practice room

Clear, achievable practice plans that make time at home feel purposeful rather than a chore.

Our space

A studio built for music

Our purpose-designed teaching room was created for one thing: helping students hear themselves and grow. Acoustic panelling keeps the sound warm and clear, and there's room to move, think and play.

  • Grand piano, regularly tuned
  • Acoustically treated walls
  • Library of graded repertoire
  • Comfortable parent waiting area
  • Easy, free street parking
  • Short drive from the city centre
