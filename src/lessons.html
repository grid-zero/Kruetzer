Lesson length

Which lesson is right for me?

Lesson length grows with the student. As repertoire gets longer and more demanding, a little more time each week makes a big difference.

Not sure? Your teacher will recommend a length after your free trial.

30 min
Beginners to Grade 4. Perfect for young students and those building early foundations.
45 min
Grades 5 – 8. More time for scales, studies and longer exam pieces.
60 min
Grade 8 and diploma. Space for full programmes and performance coaching.
Theory
30 – 45 min. Can be combined with an instrumental lesson.
