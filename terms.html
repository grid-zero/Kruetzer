Terms & Conditions

Conditions of Tuition

We are committed to providing high-quality, one-on-one music education. Our approach is professional, structured, and tailored to every individual student.

  • Lesson Duration: Length and pricing are determined by student level. Typically, 30 minutes for beginners, 45 minutes for Grades 6-8, and 60+ minutes for Diploma students.
  • Cancellations: A minimum of 24 hours' notice is required to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows are charged in full with no make-up lesson.
  • Make-up Lessons: One (1) make-up lesson is allowed per term. Evidence of misadventure or 24-hour notice is required.
  • Illness: Students who are unwell should not attend in person.
  • Practice: Regular practice is essential for progress. Limited progress due to lack of practice does not constitute grounds for refunds.
  • Safety: We hold a valid Working With Vulnerable People's Check in the ACT.

Payment Information

Lessons must be paid in advance in packages of 1, 5, 10, or per term. Payments are accepted via bank transfer, PayID, or cash. No refunds are provided if a student discontinues.
