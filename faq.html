Support

Common Questions

How do I book my first free trial lesson?

Simply head over to our contact page and fill out the enquiry form. We’ll get back to you within 24 hours to find a time that suits your schedule at our Canberra studio.

What instruments do you teach?

We currently offer professional tuition in Piano, Violin, Guitar, Voice, and Drums. Our teachers cater to all levels, from absolute beginners to advanced musicians preparing for exams.

Do I need my own instrument to start?

For your first trial lesson, we can often provide an instrument to use in-studio. However, for regular practice and progress, we recommend having your own instrument at home. We can provide advice on purchasing or renting quality equipment.

Where are the lessons located?

Our dedicated studios are located in the heart of Canberra. Once your lesson is confirmed, we will send you the specific studio address and parking instructions.

Still have questions?

We're here to help you find the right path for your musical journey.
